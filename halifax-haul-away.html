Before you choose a drop-off

  • At an eligible Halifax home, check the collection date, packaging and limits before promising curbside staging. Halifax household sorting guide
  • For contractor haul-away, ask the receiving facility about accepted material, hours, per-visit minimums and surcharges. Halifax’s council report identifies a Goodwood drop-off option for vegetative debris. Check the current terms with the facility. Halifax vegetative debris report
  • The charge per visit is the greater of the weight fee and the minimum, plus any fixed fee. Add crew and vehicle costs, then divide by one minus the target gross margin.
