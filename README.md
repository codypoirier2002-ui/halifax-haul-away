# Halifax leaf and brush haul-away cost guide

Price the cleanup on the property and the trips to unload separately. A trip costs crew time, vehicle use and disposal fees.

[Download the free calculator](https://github.com/codypoirier2002-ui/halifax-haul-away/releases/latest/download/halifax-haul-away.html) and open the HTML file in a browser. It works offline.

## Before you quote

- **Leaving material at an eligible Halifax home.** Check the collection date, packaging and limits. Halifax’s household guide lists paper yard-waste bags, bundles and the green cart as options. Agree on the scope with the customer. [Halifax household sorting guide](https://cdn.halifax.ca/sites/default/files/documents/home-property/garbage-recycling-green-cart/sortingguide_household-eng-print.pdf)
- **Taking material away.** Ask the receiving facility what it accepts from commercial haulers, its hours, minimum charge per visit, weight rate and any surcharges. Halifax’s council report documents Goodwood as a drop-off option for vegetative debris. Check current terms with the facility. [Halifax vegetative debris report](https://pub-halifax.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=3170)
- **Loads of different weights.** Work out the fee for each load when a minimum applies. A light load can still be billed at the visit minimum.

## Worked example

These are sample figures. Use the rate and minimum from your receiving facility.

| Input | Example |
| --- | ---: |
| Two trips, 35 minutes each, at CAD $48 per hour for the whole crew | CAD $56.00 |
| Two 18 km round trips at CAD $0.70 per km | CAD $25.20 |
| Two 200 kg loads at CAD $120 per tonne, with a CAD $25 minimum and CAD $3 fixed fee per visit | CAD $56.00 |
| Total haul-away cost | **CAD $137.20** |
| Haul-away price at a 25% gross margin, rounded up | **CAD $182.94 before tax** |

For a 25% gross margin, divide the cost by 0.75. Include the on-site work separately when you prepare the quote.
